Laundry Best Practices

The Three Pillars of Garment Sorting

Preserve fiber structure and prevent accidental color transfer by adopting simple sorting routines.

1. Color Grouping

Separate crisp whites, lights/pastels, and deep darks (black, navy, deep maroon). This prevents dye bleeds from turning pristine whites into cloudy greys.

Recommended: Ocean Fresh Liquid

2. Fabric Weight & Texture

Do not wash heavy denims or zip-up jackets in the same load as fine knitwear or silk scarves. Heavy zippers and coarse rivets abrade lightweight yarns during high-speed spinning.

Recommended: Floral Bloom for Delicates

3. Stain Pre-Spotting

Treat grease, curry, or collar sweat before the wash cycle begins. Dab 5ml of Citrus Sunshine liquid directly onto the stain, let sit for 10 minutes, and place directly in machine.

Recommended: Citrus Sunshine Concentrated
Textile Compatibility

Fabric-by-Fabric Care Guide

Cottons & Linens

Naturally absorbent and resilient. Wash in cold or lukewarm water (30°C - 40°C) with White Mist Ocean Fresh. Liquid detergent ensures natural breathability is preserved without stiff mineral buildup.

Silks, Chiffons & Delicates

Protein-based yarns require pH-neutral cleansers. Use White Mist Floral Bloom with gentle or hand-wash cycle. Never wring silk forcefully; roll in a dry towel to absorb moisture.

Activewear & Synthetic Blends

Polyester, nylon, and elastane trap body sebum and sweat odors. Avoid conventional wax-based fabric softeners which coat moisture-wicking pores. Use Citrus Sunshine or Ocean Fresh.

Denim & Heavy Twills

Turn jeans inside out before washing to protect indigo dye gradients from friction against drum walls. Wash in cold water on gentle spin to minimize crease marks.
